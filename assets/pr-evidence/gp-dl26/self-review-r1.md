### MAJOR

**MAJOR-1. The head-only rule silences every message behind a replayed head for up to 24 hours (a regression from d4a95db9).** MADE TO HAPPEN (P4, at the head and at d4a95db9).
- Where: gchat/adapter/internal/inbound/raise.go:102 (a held-back change is never due while waiting), with gchat/adapter/internal/server/server.go:1615-1622 (replay keeps raised and raised_end, fresh created) and raise.go:107 (an end at the retry bound is not mailed when raised is set).
- Sequence: message X waits behind text in the input box; X is mailed as waiting at 2 min; X ends at its 24 h bound (no second mail, by ruling E). The operator does what the README cause table says (gchat/README.md:369, "Clear what blocks the agent's session, then --replay") and replays X, but the box is not clear and gc refuses X again. X is now an intent head with raised set from its first life, so it is never mailed in its second life. Message Y of the same space arrives and waits behind X (behind_earlier). Y is never due while held back. Probe: 30 minutes after the replay, X at 34 POSTs, Y aged 30 min, 0 mails. The same probe at d4a95db9: 1 mail (Y at StuckAfter). Continued: at X's new bound (replay + 24 h) X ends unresolved with no mail at all, and only then is Y posted, refused and mailed, at about 24 h of age, a minute before its own bound.
- What goes wrong: ruling D's premise is that the head's mail covers the messages behind it. A head that was mailed in an earlier life (before a replay) covers nothing in this one, yet it still suppresses the mails of everything behind it. Customer messages wait up to a day with health "ready" and no mail. Separately, ruling E says the second mail is skipped only for "the bound its first mail named"; X's first mail named its first life's bound (old created + 24 h), not the replay's, and X's second life ends at the new bound unmailed. (Silence of the replayed message itself is round 1's rule (7), "keeps raised"; silence of the messages behind it is new in this round.)
- Fix sketch: treat a mark as belonging to the current life only when it is not before created (a replay sets created to now). In mailDue: a held-back change is suppressed only when its head is covered in its current life (the earliest intent change of the space has raised zero, so it will be mailed, or raised not before its created); otherwise mail the held-back change on its own. For the unresolved arm, read "the bound its first mail named" as raised not before created: `c.Raised.IsZero() || c.Raised.Before(c.Created) || c.Reason != reasonRetryBound` (raised_end still caps at one end mail). Whether a replayed message gets a fresh waiting mail of its own is a mayor call against round 1's rule (7). Test: P4 as a durable test (a replayed head refused again plus one message behind it: at least one mail within StuckAfter of the follower's arrival; the replayed head's end at the new bound mailed or a stated exception).
